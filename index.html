A Method, Not a Proposal Template

Four stages, each with a defined deliverable. You always know what you are getting and when it lands.

Step 01

Site & scope study

We walk the site, measure what exists and write down the constraints everyone already knows but nobody documented.

Step 02

Concept & costing

Two or three viable routes, each with a real bill of quantities — so the decision is made on numbers, not on optimism.

Step 03

Detailed engineering

Cross-checked drawings across all four disciplines, released on a schedule your procurement team can plan against.

Step 04

Supervision & handover

Stage inspections, snag closure and an as-built set that matches what was actually built on site.

The team stays deliberately small — senior engineers only, no drafting mill — because trust is the product, and it does not scale by headcount.
