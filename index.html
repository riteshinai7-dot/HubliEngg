From Drawing Board to Site

Four stages, each with a defined deliverable, so you always know what you are getting and when it lands.

Step 01

Site & requirement study

We visit the site, take stock of what exists and record the ground conditions and constraints the design has to work with.

Step 02

Structural design

The structural scheme is worked out and checked — framing, foundations and member sizing settled before anything is drawn for issue.

Step 03

Structural drawings

RC drawings and beam layouts issued sheet by sheet, in a form your contractor and bar bender can build directly from.

Step 04

Site consulting

Structural consulting works continue through execution — queries resolved on site so what gets built matches what was designed.
